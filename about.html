About us

We work in the back room, so you can run the front.

Backroom VA is a virtual assistant agency built for busy business owners who need reliable help without the cost of a full in-house hire.

Why we started

Most business owners did not start their business to answer emails, chase invoices or update spreadsheets. Yet those tasks still have to be done, and they often land on the person with the least time to spare.

Backroom VA exists to take that load away. We give you skilled, dependable virtual assistants who handle the everyday work in the background, cutting your costs and filling the gaps in your team, so you can focus on the things that actually move your business forward.

What you can expect

  • Reliability. Work done when we say it will be, with clear updates along the way.
  • Discretion. Your business information and your clients are handled with care and confidentiality.
  • Flexibility. Support that grows or shrinks with your business, without long hiring cycles.
  • Clear communication. One point of contact and straightforward reporting on what has been done.
  • Work from anywhere. We support business owners wherever they are, fully remotely.

Who we help

Any business owner who wants to cut costs and get skilled help with admin, lead generation, marketing or account management.

Solo founders

Doing everything yourself? Hand off the admin and get hours back every week.

Growing small teams

Fill the skills gaps in your team without the cost and commitment of a new hire.

Established businesses

Add flexible capacity for busy periods, new projects or ongoing support.
